# 6. Pose & Facial Expression Detection

## Problem

**Problem Statement**

Identify the poses and facial expressions of people in an digital image of a cultural heritage object.

**Background**

Facial expressions provide clues to emotional states. Poses provide clues as to actions and emotional states.
