# 6. Iconography Classification (pixel level)

## Problem

**Problem Statement**

Identify iconographic figure(s) depicted on a cultural heritage object from a digital image of the cultural heritage object, and record pixel bounding boxes in the image for each figure.

**Related Problems**

  * Iconography Classification (image level)
