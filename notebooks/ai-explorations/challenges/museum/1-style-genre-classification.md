# 1. Style/Genre Detection

### Problem

Also known as Discipline, Period, Subject.

**Problem Statement** 

Classify a cultural heritage object by artistic style, genre and/or period from media and/or metadata

**Problem Background**

There is much confusion in the terminology here both in AI research on this topic and within cultural heritage in general, for example 'Subject' in library cataloguing is used to classify the contents of the item (e.g. Fiction) whereas in museum cataloguing it could be used to refer to some portion of the visual content of the artwork (e.g. a tree depicted in a painting). The boundaries in meaning between Style/Genre/Period/Discipline are not entirely clear (to anyone); while some terms are fairly obivously assigned to one of these (e.g. Renaissance), other terms could fall into more than one of the these areas. 

An additional complication in much of the research on AI and Cultural Heritage is the dominance of research on paintings, with the assumption that this can be applied to all other cultural heritage object types. Genre in this usage is very specifically for genres of paintings (and sometimes other decorative arts), but it is less clear what meaning it would have if applied to other artefacts such as chairs (where Type would commonly be used) or dresses (where Style would commonly be used).
