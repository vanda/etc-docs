# 2. Image Retrieval for Cultural Heritage Objects (pixel-level)

## Problem

Also known as pattern recognition

**Problem Statement**

Retrieve related images of cultural heritage objects from a source image of a cultural heritage object, based on the similiarity of a region of the image.

**Related Problems**

  * Iconography Classification (image and pixel level)
