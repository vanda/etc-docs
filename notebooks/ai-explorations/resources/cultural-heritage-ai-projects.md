# Cultural Heritage AI Projects

An attempt to record cultural heritage (primarily in the Galleries, Libraries, Archives or Museums sector) projects making innovative use of AI. Innovative in this case is defined as going beyond the use of "off the shelf" AI tools such as ChatGPT, unless the use is particularly note-worthy in itself. Some of the projects are research based but many others (especially from the late 2010s) are practical usage in the institution.
